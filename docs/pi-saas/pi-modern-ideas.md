# pi modern ideas (candidates for the library)

Written 2026-10-26 for the owner's request that pi gives modern solutions and ideas. Each idea is
ONE plain sentence, "what to do, so the benefit", an action the owner can do alone, modern in kind
(short video, shared chat, photo, online map listing, online catalogue, voice note, review, reminder),
and names no app or brand, no digit and no time word. All 30 pass `solution_rules.check_idea` and
`is_owner_action` (checked when written). They are candidates: PI-Brain merges them into
`packs/library/owner_rewrites.json` / the library by problem kind, the owner approves.

| Problem kind | Ideas |
|---|---|
| sales_conversion (people ask the rate, then go quiet) | • Send a clear photo, the size chart and one line on delivery together with the rate, so the buyer has no reason to ask again.<br>• Answer each enquiry with a short voice note as well as the text, so it feels like a person and not a list.<br>• Message the people who went quiet after the rate with your most liked piece, so the talk starts again. |
| attention_without_orders (many views, few orders) | • Put the rate and how to order in the first line of every short video caption, so a viewer can act without asking.<br>• Pin one short video at the top of your page that explains ordering, so new visitors know what to do.<br>• Ask your recent buyers which video made them order, so you can make more of that kind. |
| no_customers_yet (stock, no clients) | • Show your best five pieces in short videos with the fabric close up, so people can judge quality before they ask.<br>• Offer a small group of friends and family a first look in a private chat, so your first buyers become your first reviews.<br>• Set up an online catalogue with clear photos and rates for each piece, so people can browse and share it. |
| repeat_customers | • Ask every happy buyer for a short review with a photo and share it in your chat and page, so new customers trust you faster.<br>• Keep a list of customers who agreed to hear from you and message them when new stock arrives, so they come back first.<br>• Send a thank-you note with a small gift idea to buyers who ordered twice, so they feel known and order again. |
| footfall_local_shop | • Put your shop on the online map with photos, timings and a way to message you, so nearby people can find you.<br>• Share a short video of the shop floor and new arrivals each week, so people nearby want to visit.<br>• Reply to every review and every message from nearby people on the same day, so they choose you over the next shop. |
| cash_and_stock_loss | • Count the cash at closing with two people and post the total in one shared chat, so a gap is seen at once.<br>• Photograph the stock shelves at opening and closing and share them in the staff chat, so a missing item shows up early.<br>• Match each day's sales record against the stock count before you leave, so the difference is clear while it is fresh. |
| payments_late (milk, wholesale, dues) | • Send a polite reminder on the day it is due, so paying is easy and nobody feels chased.<br>• Keep one shared list of who owes you and what, and update it after every call, so nothing is forgotten.<br>• Agree one fixed day with your biggest buyers and confirm it in the chat, so both sides know when to settle. |
| orders_and_delivery | • Confirm every order in the chat with the item, rate and address before dispatch, so fewer parcels come back refused.<br>• Send the buyer a photo of the packed parcel before it leaves, so there is no doubt about what was sent.<br>• Share the courier tracking message with the buyer as soon as it is booked, so they stop asking where the parcel is. |
| staff_and_work | • Write the opening and closing tasks in a shared checklist and ask for a photo when done, so you can see it was done.<br>• Share the week's roster in the staff chat in advance and ask each person to reply with a thumbs up, so nobody can say they did not know.<br>• Give each recurring job one owner by name in the team chat, so nothing waits for someone else. |
| bookings_and_no_shows | • Send a reminder message the day before and ask for a yes, so empty slots are known early.<br>• Keep a short waiting list in a shared chat and message it when a slot opens, so a cancelled slot is filled.<br>• Share your booking steps in one pinned message with the timings and a way to confirm, so people book without calling. |

## How pi should use them

- Give two per reply, never one already given, never one that does not fit the noted problem.
- Use the person's own channel and product words (the Instagram DM they named, their reels, their shop).
- Never claim a result. Never name a platform unless the person did.
- Same shape as `pi-golden-replies.md`: opener, two bullets, the spec's offer, one question last.
