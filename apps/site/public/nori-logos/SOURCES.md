# nori-logos: sources

Collected 2026-11-03 for the "works beside your software" strip. All ten are the company's own files (none from simple-icons).
Every file was rendered and checked on white and on cream (#f7f1e6). Normalisation applied to every SVG: viewBox kept or added, fixed
width/height removed, XML prolog/comments/titles/scripts removed (no file contained a script). Nothing was redrawn.
`logos.json` width/height = the viewBox size, rounded.

## Read this first: permission

Most of these brand owners say their logos need written permission, or are limited to partners, and that a page must not imply
endorsement. A neutral "works with" strip is the usual case where they say to ask first. Get sign-off (or accept the risk) before
these go live, and keep the strip wording to "works beside / compatible with", never "partner" or "certified".
