pi - brand assets  (version 1.0, 3 October 2026)
================================================
Everything here is generated from one source file (brand_lib.py), the same one the brand guide uses,
so the files and the guide cannot disagree.

svg/character/   8 expressions x 4 colour modes.  pi-<expression>-<mode>.svg
                 expressions: rest (THE MAIN CHARACTER), think, done, sorry, greet, noted, unsure, heads
                 modes: color (violet), dark (lilac, for Ink grounds), ink, white
                 In ink and white the face is a true cut-out: it shows whatever is behind it.
svg/micro/       pi-micro-*  (20-31 px)  and  pi-none-*  (bubble + spark, under 20 px), plus favicon SVGs
svg/lockups/     stacked and horizontal, 4 colour modes.  Wordmark is real outlines (Jost SemiBold).
svg/wordmark/    the wordmark alone
png/character/   1024 px transparent PNGs of every expression and colour mode
png/avatar/      WhatsApp profile photo, 640 and 1024 square, light (#EEEBFE) and dark (Ink) backgrounds
png/lockups/     2000 px PNGs of the colour and dark lockups
png/favicon/     16 / 32 / 48 / 180 px plus pi-favicon.ico
stickers/        512 x 512 transparent PNG and WebP, one per expression
tokens/          pi-tokens.css and pi-tokens.json

Minimum sizes (measured at real pixel sizes): full face 32 px and up; micro face 20-31 px; bubble + spark under 20 px.
Always use "rest" unless another expression has a job to do right now.  pi is always lowercase.
Never retype the wordmark - use the outlined files.
